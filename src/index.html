Calibrate

Measures what this display actually emits, so ambient light can be matched to it.

  1. Drag this window onto the display you want to calibrate.
  2. Hold the LDR flat against the white square below, covering it.
  3. Press Ready. The display sweeps its whole range – leave it alone and keep the room's lighting steady until it finishes (~3 min).

ExtraDim

Once brightness hits 0% the display can still go darker by dropping contrast. The purple stretch of the curve is where that happens.
